## 1. Directly related: cites or surveys the paper

- **A Literature Review on the Effects of the Minimum Wage: From Employment to Well-Being** (2024, Springer book chapter).
  Survey covering employment, hours, and welfare effects of minimum wages; cites García-Morán, Jiang & Rachinger (2024).
  [Springer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-67604-8_4) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/384946586_A_Literature_Review_on_the_Effects_of_the_Minimum_Wage_From_Employment_to_Well-Being)

## 2. German minimum wage: employment and duration effects

- **Caliendo, Pestel & Olthaus — Long-Term Employment Effects of the Minimum Wage in Germany: New Data and Estimators** (Labour Economics, 2024).
  Long-run regional effects of the 2015 introduction and subsequent increases; the introduction mainly hit East Germany, later increases hit low-wage West German regions.
  [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927537124001441) · [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5015033) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.15964)

- **A 22 percent increase in the German minimum wage: nothing crazy!** (2024, arXiv).
  Evaluates the 2022 hike to €12 using ~8 million observations from the German Earnings Survey (triple-difference design). Finds no reduction in job stability or aggregate employment; negative hours effects concentrated among minijobbers. Most directly relevant follow-up to your duration/job-attachment question.
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.12608v2)

- **The Devil is in the Details: Heterogeneous Effects of the German Minimum Wage on Working Hours and Minijobs** (2024, arXiv).
  Regular employment unaffected; minijobs decline via transitions into both regular jobs and non-employment — heterogeneity analysis close in spirit to yours.
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.17206v2/)

- **Unemployment effects of the German minimum wage in an equilibrium job search model** (Labour Economics, 2024).
  Structural search-model counterpart to reduced-form duration studies.
  [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927537124001222)

- **Drechsel-Grau — Employment and Reallocation Effects of Higher Minimum Wages** (working paper).
  Reallocation of workers to more productive firms after German minimum wage increases.
  [PDF](https://www.moritzdrechselgrau.com/static/minimum-wage-paper.pdf)

- **Dustmann et al. — Reallocation Effects of the Minimum Wage** (QJE, 2022).
  The benchmark study on the 2015 German introduction: small employment effects, reallocation toward better firms.
  [Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/137/1/267/6355463)

- **The German National Minimum Wage Raised Employment Prospects of Unemployed Welfare Recipients** (Social Policy and Society, Cambridge).
  Transitions from unemployment/welfare into work — complements your low-pay duration angle.
  [Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/social-policy-and-society/article/german-national-minimum-wage-raised-employment-prospects-of-unemployed-welfare-recipients/6F6D076D7C6A7E521D4AAE7D673D5889)

- **Bossler, Liang & Schank — Effects of the German Minimum Wage on Earnings and Working Time Using Establishment Data** (JBNST, 2024).
  Same journal as your paper; ~5.3% hourly wage gains, hours cuts for minijobbers.
  [De Gruyter](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/jbnst-2024-0025/html)

- **Effects of the German Minimum Wage on Wages and Household Income** (JBNST, 2023).
  [De Gruyter](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/jbnst-2023-0024/html)

## 3. Minimum wage and gender

- **Mind the gap: effects of the national minimum wage on the gender wage gap of full-time workers in Germany** (Journal of Economic Inequality, 2025).
  The closest recent complement to your gender-heterogeneity finding, focused on full-time workers — where your job-attachment effects were strongest.
  [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10888-025-09669-6)

- **Can minimum wage increases narrow the gender wage gap? Evidence from China** (Applied Economics, 2025).
  Minimum wages narrow the gender gap at the bottom/middle of the distribution but not at the top; some negative employment effects for low-wage workers.
  [Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00036846.2024.2402953) · [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/applec/v57y2025i52p8726-8744.html)

- **Effect of minimum wage increase on gender wage gap in Mongolia** (CEU thesis, 2024).
  "Sticky floor" mechanism for gender-gap compression.
  [PDF](https://www.etd.ceu.edu/2024/enkhbayar_namuun.pdf)

## 4. Theory and meta-analytic perspectives (bridge to your MAIVE work)

- **Integrated analysis of informality, minimum wage, and monopsony power: A synthesis of meta-analyses** (2025, arXiv).

  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.20465)

- **The effect of minimum wages on employment in the presence of productivity fluctuations** (2025, arXiv).
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.18261)

- **Minimum Wages and Optimal Redistribution** (arXiv).
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00839)
