# Related Literature: Banks, Venture Capital, and Entrepreneurship

Papers scraped from the web (July 2026) related to the research of
Heiko Rachinger with **Ming-Jin Jiang** on banks in the venture capital
market and the financing of entrepreneurship
(cf. Jiang, M.-J., "Banks in the Venture Capital Market", 2015;
[ResearchGate profile](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ming-Jin-Jiang-2)).

---

## 1. Foundational papers: banks as venture capitalists

- **Hellmann, Lindsey & Puri — Building Relationships Early: Banks in Venture Capital** (Review of Financial Studies, 2008).
  The benchmark empirical paper: banks invest in VC strategically to build future lending relationships; prior VC relationships raise the likelihood of a loan and improve loan pricing.
  [RFS](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/21/2/513/1607311) · [NBER w10535](https://www.nber.org/papers/w10535) · [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=556522)

- **Winton & Yerramilli — Entrepreneurial Finance: Banks versus Venture Capital** (Journal of Financial Economics, 2008).
  Theory of when startups choose bank debt versus VC: monitoring intensity, risk, and continuation decisions.
  [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X08000135)

- **Ueda — Banks versus Venture Capital: Project Evaluation, Screening, and Expropriation** (Journal of Finance, 2004).
  Entrepreneurs trade off banks' weaker screening against VCs' expropriation risk.
  [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2004.00643.x)

- **Gilson & Black — Venture Capital and the Structure of Capital Markets: Banks versus Stock Markets** (Journal of Financial Economics, 1998).
  Why active VC markets need stock-market exit; bank-centered systems develop weaker VC.
  [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X97000457) · [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=46909)

## 2. Bank-affiliated VC: recent empirical work (2022–2025)

- **Qian et al. — From Competitors to Partners: Banks' Venture Investments in Fintech** (FDIC Center for Financial Research, 2024).
  Banks favor payments, banking & lending, and AI/cloud fintechs; bank-backed fintechs show higher IPO rates (4.6% vs 0.9%) and lower failure rates (14.6% vs 26.3%).
  [FDIC paper](https://www.fdic.gov/system/files/2024-09/qian-paper-091824.pdf)

- **Investment strategies of bank-affiliated and independent venture capitalists: innovation in fintech after the global financial crisis** (Review of Managerial Science, 2025).
  Bank-affiliated VCs picked less innovative ventures than independent VCs before the crisis but converged to independents afterwards.
  [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11846-025-00838-5)

- **Do European fintechs benefit from bank-affiliated VCs?** (International Review of Economics & Finance, 2024).
  [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/reveco/v93y2024ipbp167-188.html)

- **Banks' investments in fintech ventures** (Journal of Banking & Finance, 2022).
  Banks' strategic motives when taking equity stakes in fintech startups.
  [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S037842662200334X)

## 3. Banks vs. VC in financing entrepreneurship (2024–2026)

- **The role of venture capital and bank credit in new ventures creation** (Journal of International Entrepreneurship, 2025).
  Cross-country evidence of a substitution effect: where VC is readily available entrepreneurs avoid bank credit, and vice versa. Directly relevant to a banks-vs-VC entrepreneurship agenda.
  [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10843-025-00386-9)

- **The dynamic linkage between fintech VC funding, bank credit flows, and equity market movement: evidence from a global perspective** (Financial Innovation, 2025).
  Causal dynamics between VC financing, bank credit, and equity markets across countries.
  [Springer open access](https://jfin-swufe.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s40854-025-00791-y)

- **Fang et al. — From Banks or Venture Capitals? The Financing Choices of Chinese High-Tech Enterprises During the Start-up Stage** (2024).
  [PDF (Cardiff ORCA)](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/170233/8/fang-et-al-2024-from-banks-or-venture-capitals-the-financing-choices-of-chinese-high-tech-enterprises-during-the-start%20(1).pdf)

- **OECD — Leveraging Venture Capital for SMEs, in Financing SMEs and Entrepreneurs 2026**.
  Latest cross-country policy snapshot on VC vs bank lending for SMEs.
  [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/financing-smes-and-entrepreneurs-2026_075d8058-en/full-report/leveraging-venture-capital-for-smes_484042d0.html)

- **Venture capital in bank-based and market-based financial systems: a cross-country analysis** (2022).
  [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/362648321_VENTURE_CAPITAL_IN_BANK-BASED_AND_MARKET-BASED_FINANCIAL_SYSTEMS_A_CROSS-COUNTRY_ANALYSIS)

- **International perspectives on venture capital and bank finance for entrepreneurial firms** (Journal of Industrial and Business Economics, 2017).
  Effectiveness of the two financing arrangements depends on institutional conditions and regional context.
  [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40812-017-0069-6)

## 4. Broader context

- **Gornall & Strebulaev — Venture Capital's Role in Financing Innovation** (NBER w27492).
  What we know and what we still need to learn about VC and innovation.
  [NBER PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w27492/w27492.pdf)

- **Hamao, Packer & Ritter (2000)** on bank-affiliated VC in Japan — the classic evidence that bank-run VC funds lack the specialized skills for high-tech screening.
  (See discussion in [Gilson & Black](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=46909).)

---

### Observations

- The literature has recently pivoted from the classic "banks vs VC as financing choice" question toward **banks as equity investors in fintech** (Section 2) — the strategic-complementarity mechanism from Hellmann, Lindsey & Puri (2008) re-tested in the fintech era.
- The 2025 Journal of International Entrepreneurship paper (substitution between VC availability and bank credit in new venture creation) is the closest recent match to a banks-and-VC entrepreneurship research question and a natural citation/positioning reference.
